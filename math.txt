--- Visualization and audio only stuff ---
Blue point changes Mandelbrot set starting constant z
Red point changes Julia set starting constant z 

--- S ---
0: Off
1: Mandelbrot x values
2: Mandelbrot y values
3: Mandelbrot x & y values
4: Julia x values
5: Julia y values
6: Julia x & y values
7: All points x & y value

--- s ---
0: None
1: Both
2: Mandelbrot
3: Julia

--- p ---
0: None
1: Both
2: Mandelbrot
3: Julia

--- L_M ---
Mandelbrot set:
0: Points only
1: Draw lines

--- L_J ---
Julia sets:
0: Points only 
1: Draw lines

--- I ---
0: No initial point (line from 0th iteration to 1st iteration)
1: Yes initial point

--- n_max ---
Sets maximum iterations

--- m_max ---
Sets maximum points

--- A_s ---
How many digits of accuracy for how close a point has to be to its neighbors to be considered a discrete periodic point (eg 1 is 0.1)

--- A_sp ---
How many digits of accuracy for how flat slope has to be for it to be considered possibly periodic or Siegel disk (eg 1 is 0.1)

--- T_Ms and T_Js ---
0: Periodic
1: Siegel
2: Collapsing

Z_Mimag = (sign(-4*s_M.y) * sqrt(max(0, (sqrt((1-4*s_M.x)^2 + (-4*s_M.y)^2) - (1-4*s_M.x)) / 2))) / 2
